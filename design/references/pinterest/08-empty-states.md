# Pattern 8 — Plain-language empty states with one clear action

**Concrete visual pattern:** four parts — a simple supporting icon/illustration, a short headline saying what belongs here, one or two lines saying why it is empty and what will appear, and **one** prominent action ("Créer un dossier", "Effacer les filtres"). For "no results", the action is a visible "Clear filters" or "Reset search". Never leave a blank panel or a bare "Aucun résultat".

**Why it fits ACSF:** blank tables/dialogs look like breakage to low-literacy users on flaky connections. A clear explanation plus one action turns a dead end into recovery and teaches what the area is for. One action respects Hick's law and avoids decision paralysis.
